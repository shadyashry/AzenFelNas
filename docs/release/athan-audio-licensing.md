# Athan audio: licensing the classic Egyptian sheikhs (§15 D93)

The owner chose, on 2026-10-29, to ship recordings of the great Egyptian sheikhs instead of a commissioned muezzin. This file is the plan for clearing them. Until a written licence is signed, nothing goes into the repository: no file downloaded from YouTube, SoundCloud or another Athan app, whatever its claims about the recording.

> Not legal advice. The law summary below comes from public translations of Egypt's Law 82 of 2002. An Egyptian IP lawyer reviews the contracts before signature.

## 1. Which voices

Since January 2025 Egyptian **Quran Radio** has broadcast the five daily Athans in the voices of seven sheikhs, by decision of the chairman of the National Media Authority, Ahmed El-Moslmany ([Akhbar El-Yom](https://akhbarelyom.com/news/newdetails/4532300/1/%D8%A8%D9%82%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A3%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%AF-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%8A-%D8%A5%D8%B0%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A8%D8%A3%D8%B5)). Those recordings are therefore in the Maspero archive, broadcast quality, and already known to Egyptian listeners. Mustafa Ismail's Athan was Egyptian TV's daily Maghrib Athan from the late 1970s to the early 1990s ([Al-Araby](https://www.alaraby.co.uk/%D9%85%D8%A2%D8%B0%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%B0%D8%A9%D9%8C-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%A3%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D8%B5%D8%AF%D9%89)).

**Launch set (proposal):** the three priority-1 voices, the remaining ones as they are cleared.

**The Fajr Athan.** Fajr adds «الصلاة خير من النوم» ("prayer is better than sleep"), so each voice needs its own Fajr recording. Old recordings often exist only for one prayer. Ask the archive for each voice's Fajr Athan by name. Without one, that voice is offered for the other four prayers only and Fajr falls back to another voice. A Fajr version is never made by splicing: that would distort the performance, which the law forbids (§2), and would be wrong as a matter of religious respect.

## 2. Who holds which right (Law 82 of 2002)

Three different rights attach to one recording, and the app needs all three cleared.

1. **The recording (producer's right).** 50 years from making or first publication, whichever is earlier (Art. 167) ([Commons summary](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Copyright_rules_by_territory/Egypt), [WIPO Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/22066)). Many 1930s–1960s recordings may be out of this term, but:
   - the usable masters are in the archive, and the archive's own restorations and transfers may carry new rights;
   - so the practical licensor is whoever holds the master. For radio recordings that is the **National Media Authority (Maspero)**, which absorbed the Egyptian Radio and Television Union. For commercial records it is **Sono Cairo (Sawt El Qahira)**, a company the authority owns outright. A 2020 court ruling confirmed Sono Cairo's ownership of Umm Kulthum's catalogue, and the company has warned that use without its written licence brings civil and criminal liability ([Al-Ahram](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/2496549.aspx), [Wikipedia](https://ar.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%AA_%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A9_%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%AA%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA_%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%A6%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%AA)).
2. **The performer's moral right.** Perpetual, inalienable and held by the performer's heirs. It covers being named as the performer and preventing "any modification, alteration or distortion" of the performance (Art. 155, [Wikisource translation](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Egyptian_Copyright_Law)). Trimming silence, normalising loudness, a fade, and above all the **29-second iOS clip** are modifications. So each sheikh's heirs give written consent to those exact edits and to the way the name is shown, even when the recording itself is licensed from Maspero.
3. **Any exclusive digital licensee.** In 2019 the authority granted United Media Services' platform **Watch iT** exclusive digital exploitation rights to the Maspero archive ([Asharq Al-Awsat](https://aawsat.com/home/article/1739241/%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%84-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1-%D8%A8%D8%B9%D8%AF-%D8%AD%D8%B5%D9%88%D9%84-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%C2%ABwatch-it%C2%BB-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%82%D9%88%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%80%C2%AB%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AB-%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B1%D9%88%C2%BB)). The reports concern television. The licence from Maspero must state in writing that radio audio used in a mobile app is outside that exclusivity, or Watch iT signs too.

**Families.** Muhammad Rifaat's family is active: in 2025 his granddaughter gave Maspero recordings made in 1934 from the family of Zakaria Pasha Mehran ([Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2025/2/6/%D8%AA%D8%B0%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B1%D9%85%D8%B6%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%84%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%AE-%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%AF-%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B9%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%AF%D9%8A-%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B1%D9%88-%D8%AA%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA/6874211)), and the family has said it collected 60 hours of unpublished recordings ([Sky News Arabia](https://www.skynewsarabia.com/varieties/1426014-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%88%D8%B1-%D9%82%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A7-%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%B9-60-%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%AB-%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%84-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%AE-%D9%85%D8%AD%D9%85%D8%AF-%D8%B1%D9%81%D8%B9%D8%AA)). The other families are reached through Quran Radio or the Reciters' Syndicate (نقابة القراء).

## 3. Religious review

Whether a deceased sheikh's recorded Athan may be used is debated in Egypt for **mosques**: Egypt's Grand Mufti saw no objection to the unified recorded Athan, while preferring a living muezzin as recommended practice rather than a condition of validity ([Dar al-Ifta](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/articles/details/8088/%D9%85%D9%81%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%85%D9%87%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B9-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D8%B9%D8%A7-%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%AF-%D9%88%D9%8A%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%BA%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B6%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%86)); Dr Ahmed Karima opposed broadcasting the voices of departed sheikhs ([Masrawy](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2021/10/11/2103885/%D9%83%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%AD%D8%AF-%D9%85%D8%AE%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A8-%D8%A5%D8%B4%D9%83%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%85)). The app's Athan is a personal reminder, not the mosque's call, but that judgement belongs to the Sharia board (§0.2 rule 1). The board's written sign-off is a release gate for this feature, next to the profile approvals.

## 4. What to ask for (term sheet)

- **Recordings:** each sheikh's Athan for Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha (one take serves all four) and his Fajr Athan; the best available master (uncompressed WAV, 44.1 or 48 kHz) and the archive's recording date and reference number for each.
- **Grant:** non-exclusive; worldwide; for a mobile app on Android and iOS, bundled offline in the app and delivered as signed data updates; played as the user's prayer alarm and notification sound and previewed in the app. No exclusivity is needed and none is paid for.
- **Term:** perpetual, or at least 10 years with renewal. Installs made during the term keep working after it ends.
- **Edits (heirs' written consent, §2):** trimming leading and trailing silence; loudness normalisation to −16 LUFS; fade in and out under 1 s; encoding to AAC; a clip of at most 29 s from the start for iOS notifications (the system's limit). No other change, no splicing, no music, no effects.
- **Attribution:** the sheikh's name in Arabic and English in the sound picker and on the app's licences screen, in a form the heirs approve; "Courtesy of the National Media Authority" or the credit Maspero requires.
- **Warranties:** the licensor owns or controls the recording rights; it confirms whether any third-party exclusive (Watch iT) applies; indemnity against third-party claims.
- **Fee:** a one-off fee per recording is preferred to a per-download royalty, because the app is free and counts no downloads per sound.
- **Paperwork back to us:** the signed licence, the heirs' consent letters and the provenance sheet per recording. Their reference goes into each sound's `licenceRef`.

## 5. Delivery into the app

Per DESIGN.md (`athan_audio_manifest`, REQ-ALM-034):

1. Receive the masters; keep them outside the repository (they are licensed, not open).
2. Trim, normalise to −16 LUFS, fade; encode AAC-LC 96 kbps `.m4a` (Android, full Athan) and a `.caf` clip ≤ 29 s (iOS notification sound).
3. Write one manifest entry per sound: `soundId` (e.g. `rifaat_rast`), `file`, `durationMs`, `fajrVariantOf` for the Fajr take, `licenceRef`, `sha256`.
4. Add a row per recording to `LICENSES.md` with its licensor and licence reference.
5. Show the sheikh's name in the Athan sound picker with a preview, in Arabic and English.

## 6. Order of work

1. An Egyptian IP lawyer reviews this plan and the letter below (one meeting).
2. Send the letter to the National Media Authority, addressed to its chairman, copied to the head of Quran Radio.
3. In parallel, contact each launch-set family (Rifaat, Ali Mahmoud, Mustafa Ismail) for consent to the edits and the attribution.
4. Ask the Sharia board for its sign-off (§3).
5. On signature, deliver the masters and follow §5.
